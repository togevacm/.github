# 🌍 Togeva — Empowering Innovation through Community and Mentorship

---

## ✨ Overview

Togeva bridges the gap between high unemployment and the growing demand for skills in Africa by connecting experienced professionals with aspiring enthusiasts. Through mentorship, resource-sharing, and collaborative projects, Togeva empowers individuals to build impactful careers and drive innovation within their communities.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Africa, home to over 1.4 billion people with nearly 60% under the age of 25, faces high youth unemployment and underemployment. While the IT sector is booming and projected to generate 2.5 million jobs by 2030, many youths remain excluded due to skills gaps and lack of mentorship. Togeva addresses this by connecting professionals to learners, empowering them through structured mentorship and community-driven projects.

---

## 🎯 Vision

We help youths find purpose, discover themselves, and empower them to build meaningful careers while giving back to their communities through collaboration, innovation, and impactful solutions.

---

## 🚀 Mission

To build a thriving, supportive community that empowers individuals to take action, learn together, and create impactful solutions. Through mentorship, collaborative projects, and shared resources, we provide the tools needed to succeed and drive positive change.

---

## 🏆 Key Outcomes

- **Enhanced Skill Development**
  - Mentees gain technical and soft skills.
  - Mentors improve leadership and coaching abilities.

- **Increased Employment Opportunities**
  - More mentees secure internships, jobs, or freelance gigs.

- **Stronger Professional Networks**
  - A robust community sharing resources and knowledge.

- **Successful Project Outcomes**
  - Real-world projects with measurable impact.

- **Equity in Mentorship**
  - Access for underrepresented groups (e.g., women, rural youth).

## About Togeva

Togeva is an education-technology organisation based in Cameroon. We blend **AI insights with human mentors** to help students discover their strengths, choose meaningful paths, and practise on projects that matter.

Africa is home to more than 1.4 billion people, nearly 60% of them under 25. The opportunity is enormous — but too many young people are held back by a lack of direction, a lack of mentorship, and a lack of anything concrete to show an employer. Togeva exists to close that gap.

> We help youths find purpose, discover themselves, and empower them to build meaningful careers while giving back to their communities through collaborative, innovative, and impactful solutions.

**Our approach is practical, inclusive and community-first** — practice over theory, and community impact over credentials alone.

| Pillar | What it means |
| :-- | :-- |
| 🧭 **Clear direction** | Personalised, AI-guided orientation that maps a student's strengths, interests and goals onto real study and career options. |
| 🤝 **Confidence with support** | One-on-one mentorship, honest feedback and accountability from people already doing the work. |
| 🛠️ **Real-world proof** | Every learner finishes with a portfolio — projects built, shipped and defended in front of an audience. |

| **Student Ambassadors** | Campus leads | Students who bring Togeva's programmes to their own schools and communities. |

## STEAM Summer School 2026 — student showcase

> **Learn. Create. Innovate.**

Our 2026 STEAM Summer School, run **in partnership with LTC**, took learners aged 7–18 through robotics, Scratch, Python, JavaScript, AI and design across three tracks — *Junior Explorers* (7–10), *Young Innovators* (11–14) and *Future Builders* (15–18).

The web development track ended with three pages built from scratch. **No frameworks, no build step, no JavaScript** — two files each, laid out entirely with flexbox, then deployed live on GitHub Pages. Every repository below is the students' own work.
